Timing:
Outer steady_clock measures end-to-end search and result retention.
Host steady_clock phases measure flatten/norm, synchronized H2D, D2H, CPU Top-K.
CUDA events measure the kernel interval; stop event synchronized and checked.
End-to-end includes allocation, events, synchronization and scratch/resource cleanup.
Phases do not include all overhead and need not sum to total.
Warm-up/input generation/insertion/scalar checks/checksums are excluded.
QPS and comparisons use end-to-end latency, never kernel-only latency.
